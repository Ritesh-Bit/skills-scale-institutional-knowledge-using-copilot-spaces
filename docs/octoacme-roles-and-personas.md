# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, test planning, and acceptance validation. They collaborate with Product Managers and Developers to define testable acceptance criteria and with Project Managers to schedule QA activities.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and approach (unit, integration, e2e, manual) for each release
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Lead manual QA testing and acceptance validation
- Identify quality risks and escalate blockers
- Participate in release readiness reviews and post-deploy verification
- Collaborate on Definition of Done to ensure testability

### Goals
- Ensure all features meet acceptance criteria before release
- Minimize defects reaching production
- Enable continuous delivery through efficient test automation and planning

### Typical Communication
- Weekly QA sync with PM and delivery leads
- Test plan reviews and acceptance criteria collaboration
- Release readiness sign-off and post-incident testing

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Reviews test cases and assists with unit testing strategies
- **Product Managers**: Validates acceptance criteria are testable and measurable
- **Project Managers**: Provides QA effort estimates and identifies testing dependencies

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and executive Sponsors provide business context, approve strategic decisions, remove organizational blockers, and ensure projects align with business objectives. They serve as the voice of the business and ensure projects deliver measurable value.

### Responsibilities
- Define business case and success metrics for the project
- Provide executive approval and budget authority
- Remove organizational and cross-team blockers
- Participate in stakeholder reviews and milestone gates
- Escalate decisions requiring executive judgment
- Communicate project status to broader organizational leadership

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers measurable business value
- Maintain strategic alignment with company priorities
- Remove friction and accelerate delivery
- Maximize ROI and stakeholder satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based stakeholder updates
- Go/no-go decision gates
- Escalation path for business-impacting risks
- Executive steering committee meetings

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Approves project charter, milestones, and escalations
- **Product Managers**: Aligns on success metrics and business priorities
- **Developers**: May provide input on technical feasibility trade-offs

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads define technical direction, review complex design decisions, identify technical risks, and mentor the development team on architectural patterns and best practices. They ensure technical decisions support long-term scalability and maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical designs for major features
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and integration points
- Define technical standards and code quality expectations
- Mentor developers on architectural patterns and best practices
- Collaborate on capacity planning for complex work
- Participate in retrospectives to identify technical improvements
- Support dependency management and cross-team technical coordination

### Goals
- Ensure technical decisions support long-term scalability and maintainability
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Build team capability and knowledge sharing
- Enable faster delivery through technical excellence

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews
- Architecture sync meetings
- Code review feedback and guidance
- Risk register updates for technical blockers
- Knowledge-sharing sessions and mentoring

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Provides architectural guidance and code review mentorship
- **Project Managers**: Identifies technical dependencies and effort implications
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Collaborates on testability and quality approaches for complex features

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove impediments to team productivity, and coach the team on agile practices and continuous improvement. They enable the team to self-organize and deliver value incrementally.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove impediments and blockers to team progress
- Coach the team on agile methodologies and practices
- Maintain and optimize the sprint board and backlog
- Track team velocity and suggest process improvements
- Escalate unresolved blockers to Project Manager or Sponsor

### Goals
- Maximize team productivity and flow
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Enable consistent, predictable delivery
- Build agile capability across the team

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-ones for coaching and feedback
- Process improvement discussions in retrospectives
- Escalation summaries to Project Manager

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Escalates blockers and provides velocity/burndown data
- **Developers**: Removes impediments and coaches on agile practices
- **Product Managers**: Supports backlog refinement and prioritization ceremonies

---

## Communications/Marketing Lead

### Role Summary
Communications and Marketing Leads manage external announcements, release notes, stakeholder messaging, and customer communications. They ensure consistent, clear, and timely communication about project progress and outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Draft and manage release notes and announcement copy
- Coordinate stakeholder and customer communications
- Develop launch and go-to-market messaging
- Manage communication timing and channels (email, blog, docs, etc.)
- Gather and distill project updates for external audiences
- Support knowledge transfer and documentation for customers

### Goals
- Ensure customers and stakeholders are informed and engaged
- Drive adoption and excitement for new features
- Maintain clear, accurate communication across all audiences
- Reduce support burden through proactive documentation

### Typical Communication
- Release communication planning with PM and Project Manager
- Draft review and approval with subject matter experts
- Post-launch customer communications and feedback loops
- Marketing and launch coordination meetings

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Product Managers**: Partners on messaging and launch strategy
- **Project Managers**: Coordinates communication timing and milestone announcements
- **Developers**: May gather technical details for documentation and FAQs
